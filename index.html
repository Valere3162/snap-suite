FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before choosing a plan.

Can I use each product separately? +
Yes. Every product is fully independent — separate repo, separate API key, separate billing. Snap Suite is a bundle offer for teams that want all five. You can sign up for just snapog, just pdfsnap, or any combination you need.
What if I only need one or two of the APIs? +
Buy only what you need. The Snap Suite bundle pricing becomes meaningful at 3+ products — at that point you save up to 30% vs. individual Starter plans. If you're on the fence, start with one API and upgrade to the bundle later; we'll credit any time remaining on individual subscriptions.
Is there an annual billing discount? +
Yes. Annual plans are 2 months free (roughly 17% off) on all individual products and on the Snap Suite bundle. Founding-member customers who join the waitlist early will have annual pricing locked for life.
Do the free tiers require a credit card? +
No credit card required for any free tier. You get a live API key immediately. When you hit the free-tier ceiling, you'll be prompted to upgrade — no surprise charges.
What's the uptime / reliability guarantee? +
All products are deployed on Cloudflare's global edge network across 300+ data centers. Business-tier customers get a contractual 99.9% uptime SLA. Starter tier has best-effort reliability (historically >99.5%).
Can I switch products or cancel at any time? +
Yes. All plans are month-to-month unless you choose annual. Cancel any time from the dashboard — no cancellation fees. If you cancel mid-cycle, you keep access until the end of the billing period.
